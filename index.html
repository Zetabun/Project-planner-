Give each thing one job

The board stays readable when the shape of an item tells you what it means.

  • Feature card = the main ideaA system, feature or outcome worth building around. Other thoughts can branch from it.
  • Sticky note = a detailA behaviour, refinement, question, rule or observation that explains a feature.
  • Checklist = implementation workConcrete steps needed to make an idea real.
  • Tags = areas of the projectUse them for Engine, Sound, Environment or whatever systems an idea touches.
  • Group area = a system boundaryLasso related items into a named system. Tap its name later to edit what belongs, move the whole system, collapse it for overview, or take manual control of its boundary.

Capture first, connect second

  • Use the Idea InboxOpen the drawer on the right, jot down rough thoughts, then drag them onto the board when you are ready to organise them.
  • Say why two things connectAfter choosing two items, Pin It asks what the relationship means.Expands intoDepends onAffectsBlocked by
  • Then make it actionableAdd due dates, stamps and checklists once an idea becomes work you actually intend to do.
  • Keep capture looseNot every thought needs a connection. If you cannot finish “this idea ___ that idea”, leave it unlinked for now.
